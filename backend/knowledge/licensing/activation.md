# Activating a Software License

## Microsoft 365 apps
1. Open any Microsoft 365 app, such as Word.
2. On Windows select File > Account; on Mac select the app name menu > Sign In.
3. Sign in with your company email address. The license activates automatically.

## Adobe Acrobat
1. Open Acrobat and select Sign In.
2. Sign in with your company email address and choose the company or school account if asked.

## "Unlicensed product" or activation errors
1. Sign out of the app, close it, and sign in again.
2. Restart the computer.
3. If the error continues, contact IT Support at support@example.com to confirm a license is assigned to your account.

## License keys
Company licenses are assigned to your account; you do not normally need a key. Never share license keys, and never use keys from unofficial sources.
