# Buying a Software License

## How to request a license
1. Submit a software license request through the IT portal.
2. Include the product name, number of users, and a short business reason.
3. If the product requires approval, your manager is asked to approve the request.
4. After approval, IT purchases the license and assigns it to your account.

## Prices
Prices come from the company license catalog. The assistant only quotes prices from that catalog.

## Rules
Do not buy software licenses with personal payment cards. Purchases are only complete once IT confirms by email. For questions, contact IT Support at support@example.com.
