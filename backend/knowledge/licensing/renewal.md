# License Renewal

## Automatic renewal
Most company software subscriptions are renewed centrally by IT once a year. You do not need to do anything.

## Expiry notices
You may receive an email about 30 days before a license expires. If the software is still needed, no action is required unless the email asks you to confirm.

## License expired message
If an app says your license has expired, sign out and sign in again. If the message stays, contact IT Support at support@example.com.

## Cancelling a license
If you no longer need a license, tell IT Support so it can be reassigned.
