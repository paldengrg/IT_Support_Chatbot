# Account and Password Problems

## Forgot your password
1. Go to the Microsoft 365 sign-in page and enter your company email address.
2. Select Forgot my password.
3. Verify your identity with the method you registered (for example, the authenticator app or a text message).
4. Choose a new password that follows company password rules.

## Account locked
Accounts lock after too many failed sign-in attempts. Contact IT Support at support@example.com to unlock it. IT will verify your identity first.

## New phone or lost MFA device
You cannot reset MFA yourself. Contact IT Support at support@example.com to re-register your authentication method.

## Safety
IT Support will never ask for your password. Never share your password or MFA codes with anyone.
