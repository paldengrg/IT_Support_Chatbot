# Setting Up Company Email

Company email uses Microsoft 365. Sign in with your full company email address.

## Outlook for Windows
1. Open Outlook.
2. Select File > Add Account (or follow the welcome screen on first launch).
3. Enter your company email address and select Connect.
4. Sign in with your company password and approve the MFA prompt.

## Outlook for Mac
1. Open Outlook.
2. Select Outlook > Settings > Accounts, then click +, then New Account.
3. Enter your company email address and follow the prompts.

## Phone
Install the Microsoft Outlook app from the App Store or Google Play and add your company email account.

## Problems
If Outlook keeps asking for your password, sign out of all Microsoft 365 apps, restart the device, and sign in again. If it continues, contact IT Support at support@example.com.
