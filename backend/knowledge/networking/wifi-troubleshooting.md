# Wi-Fi Troubleshooting (All Devices)

## Basic checks
1. Make sure airplane mode is off.
2. Turn Wi-Fi off and on again.
3. Restart your device.
4. Move closer to the wireless access point.

## Forget and reconnect
Remove the saved network from your device, then connect again and re-enter the password. See the Windows or macOS articles for exact steps.

## Home router
If you are working from home, unplug the router for 30 seconds, plug it back in, and wait two minutes before reconnecting.

## When to contact IT
If other people nearby also cannot connect, there may be an outage. Contact IT Support at support@example.com and say which building or location you are in.
