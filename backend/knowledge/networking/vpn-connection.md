# Connecting to the Company VPN

## Before you start
1. Make sure your internet connection works by opening any website.
2. Make sure your computer's date and time are correct; wrong time settings can cause VPN sign-in failures.

## Connect
1. Open the company VPN client installed by IT.
2. Sign in with your company account.
3. Approve the multi-factor authentication (MFA) prompt on your phone.
4. Wait until the client shows Connected.

## VPN will not connect
1. Quit the VPN client completely and open it again.
2. Restart your computer.
3. Try a different network (for example, a phone hotspot) to rule out a blocked network.
4. Write down any error message or code.

## When to contact IT
If the VPN client is missing, needs reinstalling, or still fails, contact IT Support at support@example.com with the error message. Reinstalling the VPN client may require administrator authorization.
